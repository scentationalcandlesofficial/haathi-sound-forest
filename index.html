Learning engine: how the next chapter was chosen

    The app tracks every skill below on this phone. Skills move up a level after mostly-right first tries, and down a level after repeated misses. Each new chapter mixes a new letter, the weakest skills, and older skills due for review.

    Letter sounds

    Mastered = 4 of the last 5 first tries right, in 2 games, on 2 different days.

    Sound Studio: record real letter sounds

    Until a sound is recorded, the games use the picture word instead (for example "sun" for s), because computer voices say single sounds wrongly. Record in a quiet room. Say only the sound, short and clean, with no "uh" after it. On Android, "Record" opens the phone's recorder. On iPhone, record in Voice Memos first, save it to Files, then choose it here.

    Screen time: WHO and the Indian Academy of Pediatrics suggest no more than 1 hour a day of screen time at this age. A good daily dose here is 10 to 15 minutes, then do the writing step on paper or in a sand tray.
